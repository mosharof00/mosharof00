## Featured work

### FoodX — Multi-Vendor Food Delivery Platform

A complete food-delivery ecosystem connecting customers, restaurants, riders, and administrators.

- Built three production Flutter apps with supporting backend services.
- Engineered nearest-rider auto-dispatch with cascading accept/decline offers.
- Implemented live GPS order tracking, routing, dynamic ETAs, payments, notifications, and multi-zone operations.
- Productized for commercial distribution on CodeCanyon; deployed as the Meeza Food Dash white-label application.

[Product](https://foodx.arcadexit.com/) · [CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/item/foodx-multi-restaurant-food-delivery-app-with-admin/61272196?s_rank=1) · [Google Play deployment](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.meeza.meeza_user_app&hl=en) · [App Store deployment](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meeza-food-dash/id6758754211)

### Lovelynk — Flutter + Native iOS Widget Platform

A couples companion application with **12 native Home and Lock Screen widgets**.

- Flutter owns the product experience and application logic; SwiftUI and WidgetKit render native widgets.
- Synchronizes typed data and styles through iOS App Groups and shared storage.
- Uses feature-first architecture, GetX dependency injection, repository boundaries, Hive, secure storage, and responsive reusable UI.
- Native widgets remain useful while the Flutter application is suspended.

[Source and architecture](https://github.com/mosharof00/Lovelynk)

### Smart Academy — Learning Management System

A multi-platform learning ecosystem for students, instructors, and administrators.

- Course sales, quizzes, certificates, instructor workflows, community features, realtime chat, and push notifications.
- Resumable video learning with per-user playback progress.
- Secure payment integrations and complete marketplace-ready product delivery.
- Published commercially with **100+ products sold** and a **4.9+ marketplace rating**.

[Product](https://smartacademypro.arcadexit.com/) · [CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/item/smart-academy-complete-learning-management-system/58904031?s_rank=1)

### WorkShiftly — Workforce Shift Management

A role-based hiring and workforce platform for employers, workers, and administrators.

- Shift publishing, applications, shortlisting, hiring, payments, messaging, and realtime status tracking.
- Location-aware matching and flexible multi-role shift scheduling.
- Designed concurrent booking and availability flows without requiring a dedicated WebSocket server.

[Product](https://workshiftlypro.arcadexit.com/)

### Import Mark — B2B Trading Operations Platform

A full-stack **Next.js + Supabase** system for wholesale construction-chemical operations.

- Admin, Manager, and Customer portals for catalog, imports, inventory, orders, payments, dues, and reporting.
- PostgreSQL Row-Level Security, role-aware authentication, audit-friendly workflows, and realtime notifications.
- Structured as a production business system rather than a demonstration dashboard.

[Source and documentation](https://github.com/mosharof00/Import-Mark-Web)

A customer, vendor, and delivery-agent ecosystem covering catalog, multi-vendor checkout, order management, payments, and live delivery updates.
